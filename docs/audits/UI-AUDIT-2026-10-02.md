# UI Audit — Production (`finance.investdx.biz.id`)

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Scope:** every route in `apps/web/app` — `/`, `/login`, `/invite`, `/inbox` (+ aliases `/reviews`, `/actions`), `/transactions`, `/analytics`, `/wealth`, `/documents`, `/household`, `/settings`, `/admin`, `/privacy`, `/terms`.
**Account:** signed in as the workspace owner (Super admin), data as of 2 Oct 2026.

## How it was tested (read this before trusting a result)

| Target | Method | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Web (desktop) | Real Chrome, ~1400–1920px wide | High |
| Tablet | Real Chrome window at 768px | High. The app uses its phone layout (bottom tab bar) at this width. |
| Phone | Chrome window at 500px (its minimum), then **CSS-zoom emulation** of 375px and 393px with width media queries rewritten to match | Medium. Tests layout, wrapping and overflow only. Not real Safari, no touch, no safe-area or address-bar behaviour. |
| Public pages on phone/tablet | Built-in browser pane with true 375×812 / 768×1024 emulation | High |

Target devices named by the owner: **iPhone 14 Pro Max (430px)** and **iPhone 15 (393px)**.
**Not tested:** 430px, landscape, real Safari, the "Lainnya" menu contents, keyboard focus order and focus styles, state-changing flows (add transaction, upload, deactivate member), `/inbox` with real items (it was empty).

Findings that were wrong in earlier notes and are **dropped**: `/inbox` and `/actions` are intentional aliases (`/reviews` redirects to `/inbox?view=transactions`); signed-out `/login` is a proper page (the blank screen was the signed-in redirect state); the "tiled" `/invite` card was a screenshot artifact.

## Re-audit after the final deploy (2026-10-02, production `c1abf6b`) and round 6

A second audit of production at desktop, tablet and phone found the items below. The first audit's overflow check skipped anything inside a scroll container, so scroll regions were audited separately: every scroller, every `overflow: hidden` region that could cut content off, drawers and dialogs, and clearance above the fixed nav.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| S1 | The document drawer's extraction `<pre>` blocks scroll sideways but had no `tabindex`, label or edge cue, so keyboard users could not scroll them and touch users had no hint that text was cut off. They are now a named, focusable region (`aria-label="Hasil ekstraksi, geser untuk semua isi"`) with an edge shadow while they overflow. Verified with real keyboard input: Tab reaches the block, it shows the 3px focus ring, and the arrow keys scroll it. |
| S2 | Two table wrappers (`Nilai pengeluaran harian` on `/analytics` and `Arus kas per bulan` in the calendar view) scrolled without `tabindex`, `role="region"` or a label. They now match their siblings, and the "Transaksi pendukung" label also says the table scrolls. |
| Guard | The visual smoke now fails when any scrolling region cannot be focused or has no name (`unreachableScrollers`). It opens every `<details>`, opens the document drawer and feeds a long extraction line so the regions really overflow. It was shown to fail on the previous code, naming both `div.review-table-wrap` and `pre`. A unit lock walks the whole `app` tree for `review-table-wrap` wrappers. |

Checked and fine: no clipped content at any of the three sizes; drawers have `role="dialog"`, move focus inside and close on Esc; the add-transaction dialog fits the viewport; at least 32px of clearance above the nav on tablet; settings anchors land the section 66px from the top; the admin tab row is a labelled, focusable scroller with an edge cue on every tab.

Still open from the re-audit: raw document-type titles on `/documents` (`TRANSFER_PROOF` and others), raw `MUTUAL_FUND` / `CASH_MOVEMENT` on `/settings`, 11px chart axis ticks and the empty area under the dashboard's daily chart, 11.67px `<small>` text on `/admin`, a few small click targets, the phone login form sitting at the fold, and a cramped file picker on tablet. `/readyz` still returns 404 from outside and is unconfirmed. Not tested: iOS momentum scrolling, scroll-lock behind drawers, and nested scrolling on a real touch device.
